How PicDecoder actually works

PicDecoder tries two things, in order, and always tells you which one produced your result:

1. Reading the file's own metadata (exact)

Tools like Automatic1111, ComfyUI and most Stable Diffusion front-ends save the prompt, negative prompt, seed and sampler settings directly inside the PNG file. If that data is present, PicDecoder reads it as-is — this is not a guess. You'll see a ✓ verified badge.

2. AI reconstruction (approximate)

Many images — especially anything downloaded from Discord, Instagram or a screenshot — have that metadata stripped out. When there's nothing embedded to read, PicDecoder sends the image to a vision AI model, which describes the subject, composition, lighting and style and writes a plausible prompt. It's a strong starting point for recreating a similar look, not a guarantee of the exact original text. You'll see a ✦ reconstructed badge.
